GPX Incline Adjuster

Rebuilds a GPX so QZ can only move your treadmill one whole percent at a time, never sooner than your "min seconds" apart at your max speed (slower running only spaces them further). A full 0→15% climb takes about 90 s at 12 mph. Descents flatten to 0%, climbs cap at your max, and every file starts from your treadmill's resting incline. Each file is replayed through a copy of QZ's own incline logic before you download it.

FIT Cadence Fix

QZ saves a run's average and max cadence at twice the scale of its second-by-second cadence, so Garmin Connect shows them doubled (282 instead of 141). This halves only those summary numbers. Every other value in the file stays exactly as QZ wrote it.
